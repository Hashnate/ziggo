A district's own base fare, for a set period

Rides and Flash parcels that start in the district between the two dates use this base fare while it is switched on. Before and after the period the national base fare applies again by itself. Per-km, per-minute and minimum fares stay national, so on very short trips the national minimum fare can still decide the price. A trip keeps the base fare it was booked with, even if the period ends or is switched off before it finishes.
