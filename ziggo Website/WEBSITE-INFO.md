## 11. Help Center (pages/help.html)

**Hero:** "How can we help you?" 24/7 support.

### Topic categories
- **For riders** — bookings, payments, account, safety, lost items.
- **For drivers** — earnings, payouts, app issues, vehicle docs.
- **For partners** — onboarding, menus, orders, payouts.
- **Safety** — SOS, verification, trip sharing, incident reports.

### FAQs
- **Book a ride?** Open app → set pickup & destination → choose vehicle → confirm; nearby driver accepts and you track in real time.
- **Cancel a booking?** Open active trip → Cancel. Free within 3 min of booking or up to 5 min before driver arrival; small fee may apply after.
- **Payment methods?** Cash, credit/debit card, mobile wallets (eZ Cash, Genie, FriMi), in-app Ziggo Pay.
- **Feel unsafe?** Tap SOS — alerts emergency contacts + 24/7 safety team with live location.
- **Lost item?** App → Your trips → select trip → Find lost item; connects you with the driver.
- **Refunds?** Via Help in the relevant order; usually within 24 h to Ziggo Pay or original method.
