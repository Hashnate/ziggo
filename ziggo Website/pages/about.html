About Ziggo

We're building Sri Lanka's super app.

From a single tuk ride in 2022 to 2 million users across the island — we're just getting started.

Our story

Built in Sri Lanka, for Sri Lankans.

Ziggo started with a simple idea: getting a ride in Colombo shouldn't be hard. From there we asked — what if one app could solve more daily problems? Food. Parcels. Groceries. Trucks.

Today, 2 million people use Ziggo every day. 50,000 drivers and 10,000 merchants earn through our platform. And we're just getting started.
