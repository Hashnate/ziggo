BO-01 Provide users a fast and reliable way to book rides or become drivers on-demand. BRD Completed The platform provides an on-demand mobility service that matches riders with nearby drivers. Registration is fast, and active ride bookings complete reliably.
BO-02 Enable users to switch between customer and driver roles via a button within the app. BRD Completed The mobile app includes a quick role toggle button that allows a single user account to act as either a passenger or driver depending on active mode selection.
BO-03 Match ride requests efficiently and minimise wait times for both drivers and customers. BRD Completed An automated dispatch engine filters available drivers in a radius to match bookings quickly and keep driver wait times low.
BO-04 Ensure safe and traceable trips with ratings and trip records for both customers and drivers. BRD Completed The system logs trip records and coordinates securely, allowing riders and drivers to rate each other and review completed trip details.
BO-05 Allow operations to monitor the platform centrally via the Admin Panel. BRD Completed A central admin panel allows operators to oversee active rides, verify drivers, manage accounts, and monitor metrics.
BO-06 Support multiple payment methods (cash, digital wallet, credit/debit cards) through PayHere. BRD Completed The platform supports digital payments through PayHere, in-app wallet balances, and direct cash payments at trip completion.
BO-07 Integrate Google Maps API for accurate location tracking, routing, and real-time ETA updates. BRD Completed Map APIs are fully integrated to display maps, search addresses, calculate turn-by-turn route paths, and estimate real-time arrivals.
BO-08 Use Firebase for push notifications for ride status updates, promotions, and other relevant alerts. BRD Completed The notification system triggers push alerts for booking confirmations, driver arrivals, and promotional alerts.
BO-09 Offer multi-service delivery (food, market, parcel Flash, basic truck) from a single app. BRD Completed The single super-app vertical supports ride-hailing, package deliveries, grocery shopping, restaurant orders, and moving trucks.
BO-10 Drive repeat use through a simple loyalty points system and promo codes. BRD Completed Promos and checkout loyalty point rules drive user engagement by calculating and applying discounts to orders.
BO-11 Support basic business accounts for corporate ride and delivery bookings. BRD Completed Corporate profile configuration enables businesses to link employee profiles and charge bookings directly to company expense accounts.
BO-12 Surface third-party events to increase engagement. BRD Completed An event catalog allows users to discover local experiences and purchase tickets inside the mobile app.

BR-01 A user can only be in one active role (passenger or driver) at a time. BRD Completed Endpoints verify role sessions so users can only perform driver tasks while active in driver mode.
BR-02 Fare calculation includes base fare, distance, time, and surge pricing adjustments. BRD Completed Fare calculation logic computes booking totals using base rates, distance, time, and dynamic surge multipliers.
BR-03 Cancellation after a grace period may incur a cancellation fee. BRD Missing / Planned Cancellation grace windows are listed on the website terms, but backend systems do not charge fees or penalties for late cancellations.
BR-04 Users must be document-verified and admin-approved to operate as drivers. BRD Completed Drivers must upload valid documents and receive admin approval before they can go online.
